3. Playback

VLC displays the movie, audio tracks, subtitles and fullscreen controls. Host controls here also synchronize both players.

Timing is checked once per second. Viewers ahead by more than 3 seconds wait until others are within 1 second. Host seeks move everyone immediately. A viewer still behind after 30 seconds stops holding up the party.
